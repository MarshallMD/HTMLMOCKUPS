Assumption to confirm

I don't have the Colorway project in front of me, so the wipes here are my read of the note: three concentric circles growing from a single origin at frame centre, staggered 7 frames apart, each brand colour stacking over the last — orange → #D4D3D3 → #1D1D1D. The out is the same iris in reverse, top layer first.

If Colorway offsets the origins, uses an off-screen sweep, or runs a different colour order, tell me which and I'll re-cut it.

Wipe origin

The origin sits at 960, 540 — the exact point the globe flips in on. The colour arrives from where the mark will be, so the entrance reads as one gesture rather than two.

In AE: three shape layers, Ellipse path Size linked to one slider each, or a single Circle effect per solid with Radius keyframed. Slider is easier to re-time.

Logo is matted by wipe 3

The globe, wordmark and waves all sit inside the #1D1D1D circle as a track matte. That's what makes the reverse work for free — when the dark circle irises down, it takes the logo with it, and you're never animating logo opacity separately.

Set the content precomp's TrkMat to Alpha Matte on a duplicate of the wipe-3 shape layer.

Flip

Y rotation 180° → 0° over 26 frames on easeOutQuint, with scale 0.90 → 1.00 riding the same curve. Opacity is a 5-frame ramp only — the edge-on frames hide the pop.

Advanced 3D renderer, or just a 2D layer with a cos() scale-X expression if you want it cheap.

Shift & reveal

Globe travels −385px (960 → 575 centre) over 29 frames, easeInOutCubic. The wordmark matte opens left-to-right over 32 frames. The globe sits above the type in z-order, so the first third of the reveal happens behind the globe and the letters look like they’re being dragged out from under it.

Matte here is a plain rectangle scaling on X from the wordmark's left edge — nothing fancier is needed.

Radio wave

Emitter at the LIVE box's top-right corner, 1542.62, 456.8. Rings spawn every 19 frames, live 57 frames, radius 0 → 340 on easeOutCubic, stroke 7 → 1.5, opacity 0.85 → 0.

Masked to the upper-right quadrant of that corner, so nothing ever crosses back over the box. Holds ~3.3s before the wipe-out starts.
